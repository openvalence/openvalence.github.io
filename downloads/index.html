No hardware?

Install Phosphor and pick the hub with the "ν virtual" badge. That's the Virtual, and nothing physical moves. The Kinetic playground runs the motion planner in your browser with nothing to install. More on the demos page.

Checking a download

Only download from github.com/openvalence. This site never hosts binaries. Compare your file against SHA256SUMS.txt with sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt on Linux and macOS (shasum -a 256), or Get-FileHash in PowerShell.
