Barnacle mod

An M5Stack Stamp-P4 wired onto the OSSM R+D reference board, rev 2.3. The P4 runs Nucleus and sends A/B quadrature straight to the servo drive. The board's ESP32-WROOM-32D stays on as an I/O helper.

One 7-pin header on the Stamp and eight wires. The R+D board gets no new parts.

WIP Firmware. nucleus-p4b, the Nucleus build for the barnacle, and the WROOM shim firmware are not released yet. Status

What it is

The Stamp-P4 takes over motion. Three wires (A, B, GND) carry A/B quadrature from the P4's LP core to the servo drive's pulse and direction inputs. The servo's 10-pin signal cable no longer plugs into the R+D board.

Five more wires go to J8 on the R+D board, the 10-pin header the servo cable used: 5 V, GND, a serial link each way, and TRIP. The WROOM runs the shim firmware. It reads motor current, motor voltage and the button, drives the RGB LED, and reports to the P4 at 115200 baud.

Motor power stays on J2 SERVO POWER. The current sensor sits in that path, so the helper sees motor current only while the servo draws from J2.

Stopping the machine

The R+D board has no motor power switch and no Modbus link to the drive, and in this build the drive's enable line, ENA, is unconnected. Software cannot cut motor power.

Two things stop the motor: the P4 stops its A/B output, or the E-stop cuts the servo's supply. Wire a latching E-stop that breaks the servo's DC supply (step 5).

Overcurrent protection stays with the servo drive. The current and voltage the helper reports are for monitoring and homing.

Tools

  • Soldering iron and solder.
  • A crimp tool for JST XH, and one for the J8 plug's crimps.
  • Wire strippers.
  • A multimeter with a DC milliamp range.
  • A computer, a USB-C cable and the USB to RS485 adapter.

Build

0. Set up the servo drive

The R+D board has no Modbus link to the drive, so set the drive up first, over its own RS485 port (Modbus RTU), with the USB to RS485 adapter. Set the command input to A/B pulse input. In pulse and direction mode the drive misreads quadrature.

The servo setup tool finds the drive at any baud rate and applies the barnacle's settings. It runs in Chrome or Edge on a desktop, and it is WIP. Unplug the adapter when you are done: the barnacle does not use RS485.

1. Prep the Stamp

Plug the Stamp-AddOn C6 into the SDIO connector, at the lower left of the Stamp's top side, beside the USB-C.

Find pad 1: top left, at the camera connector end. The left column runs 1 to 19, top to bottom. On that column the odd pads are through-hole and the even pads are castellated only. The header uses the through-hole pads, so it solders like any pin header.

Pad 15 is the Stamp's USB-C VBUS. From there 5 V passes an overvoltage switch (AW32901) and a CH213K ideal diode to the module's 5 V rail, and an SY8089 buck, rated 600 mA, makes 3.3 V. So 5 V can come in on pad 15.

2. Solder the header

  1. Fit the 7-pin XH header from the back of the Stamp, into pads 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17 and 19. On the top side its body would sit on the SDIO connector.
  2. Solder the pins from the top.
  3. Check for bridges to the castellated pads between the pins: 8, 10, 12, 14, 16 and 18.

The through-hole pads sit 2.54 mm apart and JST XH pitch is 2.50 mm. Over 7 pins that leaves each end pin 0.12 mm off its pad's center, and the header goes in.

3. Build the servo loom

Three wires, under 15 cm, from the XH housing to the drive's signal input.

  • Pad 9, A, to PUL+.
  • Pad 11, B, to DIR+.
  • Pad 13, GND, to PUL− and DIR−, jumpered together at the drive.
  • ENA, ALM and PED stay unconnected.

The drive's opto inputs take 3.3 V straight from the P4, with no level shifter. That works on the bench and over 3 m of shielded Cat5 from an ESP32-S3. The P4's pins drive the same as the S3's: 20 mA by default, 40 mA source and 28 mA sink at drive strength 3.

Pad 13 takes two ground wires, this one and J8 pin 1 from the R+D loom. Crimp both into one contact if they fit, or crimp one wire and splice the other onto it close to the housing.

J8 pins 7 and 9 sit between 5 V pins (6, 8 and 10). A crimp one position off puts 5 V on a P4 pin, and the P4 does not survive it. Measure before the Stamp is plugged in (step 6).

Fit the capacitor at the Stamp end, across 5V and GND, within a few centimeters of the housing, minus lead to GND. It carries the Wi-Fi transmit bursts.

5. Wire the E-stop

Put the latching E-stop in the servo's DC supply, between J2 SERVO POWER and the drive, so pressing it breaks the supply to the drive. Keep the motor supply on J2: the current sensor sees only what flows through it.

6. Pre-power checks

  1. With nothing powered, check continuity on every wire, end to end, against the point-to-point table.
  2. Check for shorts between neighboring header pins, most of all 15 (5V) and its neighbors 13 (GND) and 17 (TX).
  3. Leave the loom off the Stamp and power the R+D board. At the XH housing, measure to GND: the 5V contact reads about 4.5 V. TX, RX and TRIP read 3.3 V or less. Anything near 5 V on a signal means a crimp sits on the wrong J8 pin.
  4. Confirm the drive is in A/B pulse input mode (step 0) with ENA, ALM and PED unconnected.

7. First power-on and current check

  1. Unplug USB-C from the Stamp.
  2. Put the meter's milliamp range in series with the 5V wire and plug the loom into the Stamp.
  3. Power the R+D board and read the Stamp's draw with Wi-Fi active. About 350 mA average is expected, with bursts near 600 mA. Well above that, the R+D 5 V rail has no room left: stop and find out why.
  4. Take the meter out, reconnect the 5V wire, then power the servo.

Power budget

The Stamp takes 5 V from the R+D board's 5 V rail: an LMR14006 buck rated 0.6 A, after a B1100 Schottky diode, so about 4.5 V at J8. The board's own loads share it. These numbers are estimates; step 7 measures the largest.

The average fits. The 220 to 470 µF capacitor at the Stamp carries the short peaks.

Do not power the Stamp from the R+D board's 3.3 V. That rail comes from an ME6211C33 regulator in SOT-23-5, which would dissipate about 0.4 W at the Stamp's load, more than that package can shed.

Pad 15 is the same net as the Stamp's USB-C VBUS. With the J8 loom connected, a computer on the Stamp's USB-C also powers the R+D board's 5 V loads. Unplug the loom before you flash over USB, or flash over Wi-Fi.

How the link works

The link uses only parts already on the R+D board.

  • TX, P4 to WROOM: the P4 drives J8 pin 2 directly. On the board that is the old ALM input, a 10 kΩ pull-up to 3.3 V into WROOM IO13. Plain 3.3 V UART.
  • RX, WROOM to P4: the WROOM's UART TX on IO14 drives Q5, whose collector is J8 pin 9. The transistor inverts, so the shim inverts its TX and the wire carries normal UART. The link runs at 115200 baud: the internal pull-up is weak, and that rate leaves wide margin on the rising edges while still carrying current and button data at 500 Hz.
  • TRIP: the WROOM drives Q4 from IO27, whose collector is J8 pin 7. On a current spike, the end stop during homing or a jam, the WROOM pulls TRIP low and the P4 stops on its next tick without waiting for the serial link.
  • While the WROOM resets or boots, IO27 floats, Q4 stays off and TRIP reads high: not tripped. A helper that hangs with IO27 high holds TRIP low, and the machine stops.
  • J8 pin 4 (WROOM IO4, input) and pin 5 (Q3, from IO26) are spare.

Troubleshooting

The Stamp does not power up
Measure pad 15 to pad 13: about 4.5 V. No voltage: check the J8 pin 10 crimp and the GND wire to J8 pin 1.
A signal contact reads near 5 V
A crimp sits one position off on J8, onto pin 6, 8 or 10. Fix it before the Stamp goes on.
The motor does not move
Check the drive is in A/B pulse input mode (step 0) and that PUL− and DIR− are jumpered to GND at the drive.
The motor runs the wrong way
A and B are swapped. Swap the two wires at the drive.
TRIP reads tripped all the time
With the helper running, J8 pin 7 idles at 3.3 V. Low: the shim holds IO27 high or has hung, or the pin 7 crimp is wrong.
No data over the link
Check TX on pad 17 goes to J8 pin 2 and RX on pad 19 to J8 pin 9. Both ends run 115200 baud, and the shim inverts its TX.
Motor current reads zero
The servo draws its power from somewhere other than J2. Move it to J2 SERVO POWER.
The Stamp resets when Wi-Fi transmits
The 5 V sags on transmit bursts. Check the bulk capacitor is fitted at the Stamp end and its polarity is right.
The R+D board lights up while you flash the Stamp
USB-C on the Stamp feeds the R+D 5 V rail through the loom. Unplug the loom, or flash over Wi-Fi.
The WROOM will not flash
Flash it over the R+D board's USB-C. The CP2102 there owns UART0.

Status WIP

The firmware is WIP. Neither piece is released yet.

  • nucleus-p4b, the Nucleus build for the barnacle: A on LPG1, B on LPG0, TRIP on LPG8 (active low), the link on G27 (TX) and G26 (RX) at 115200 baud, internal pull-ups on RX and TRIP.
  • The WROOM shim firmware: streams button, current and voltage to the P4, drives the RGB LED on request, and pulls TRIP on a current spike, with thresholds the P4 sets for homing and for running.
  • The servo setup tool for step 0.
