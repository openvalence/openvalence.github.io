ESPConnect
  The flasher's flow (confirm, write with progress and cancel, session log), its
  manual download-mode help, offsets read from the board's partition table and
  the boot slot readout follow ESPConnect. No ESPConnect source is copied; the
  otadata reader in image.js is written from ESP-IDF's bootloader_support.
  Source: https://github.com/thelastoutpostworkshop/ESPConnect
